# Motoring lesson research note

Step 4A used the accepted Practice question prompts and explanations as the
first consistency constraint. The supplied ASA Motoring Fundamentals chapter
noted in the project brief was not present in the repository or the available
attachments, so it could not be used for coverage or originality comparison.
Lesson prose is original and intentionally compact.

This note records primary or authoritative sources used for claims that extend
beyond the accepted question explanations. It is not a bibliography for every
basic seamanship statement.

| Lesson / claim | Authoritative source | How the source was used |
|---|---|---|
| Before Getting Under Way — brief passengers, identify safety gear, assign line/fender roles, and avoid using hands or feet to fend off | Oregon State Marine Board, [Docking Etiquette](https://www.oregon.gov/osmb/boater-info/Pages/Docking.aspx) | Confirmed the practical scope of a pre-departure passenger briefing and line-handling cautions. |
| Before Getting Under Way — stow lines and fenders once clear | Texas Parks & Wildlife Department, [Texas Boater Safety Course manual](https://tpwd.texas.gov/education/boater-education/tpwd-boating-safety-manual-2026-v1-2-3.pdf), p. 56 | Confirmed the after-casting-off stowage and no-wake departure routine. |
| Engine Basics — powered ventilation warning for enclosed gasoline engine spaces | U.S. Coast Guard, [A Boater’s Guide to the Federal Requirements for Recreational Boats](https://uscgboating.org/assets/1/AssetManager/Boaters-Guide-to-Federal-Requirements-for-Receational-Boats-20231108.pdf), ventilation section | Confirmed that the federal warning label on applicable boats says to operate the blower at least four minutes and check the bilge for gasoline vapors before starting. The lesson still defers to posted and manufacturer procedures. |
| Controls & Instruments — tachometer, oil pressure, coolant temperature, charge indications, and alarm response | YANMAR Marine International, [GM Series Operation Manual](https://www.yanmar.com/marine/wp-content/uploads/2020/05/0AGMM-EN0013_2018.04.pdf), pp. 18–19 | Confirmed operator-level meanings of the common indicators and the need to stop operation for low oil pressure. Exact thresholds and diagnosis were deliberately omitted. |
| Outboards — engine-cutoff-switch purpose and U.S. operating requirement | U.S. Coast Guard, [Engine/Propulsion Cut-Off Devices](https://uscgboating.org/recreational-boaters/engine-cut-off-devices.php) and [FAQ](https://uscgboating.org/recreational-boaters/engine-cut-off-switch-faq.php) | Confirmed that the link stops propulsion if the operator is displaced and that covered recreational vessels require its use while on plane or above displacement speed. The lesson avoids implying that every sailboat/outboard is covered in every operating condition. |
| Outboards — neutral start, low-speed shifting, cooling indicator, and model-specific tilt/trim limits | Honda Marine, [BF8D–BF20D Owner’s Manual](https://cdn.powerequipment.honda.com/marine/pdf/manuals/00X31ZY06450.pdf), pp. 23–35 and 60–68 | Confirmed that this representative manufacturer requires neutral for starting, directs shifting at slow throttle, treats cooling-indicator flow as evidence of circulation, and warns that excessive tilt can uncover intakes and cause overheating. The lesson says “where fitted” for trim and defers exact controls and limits to the specific manual. |
| Fueling — visible sheen reporting | U.S. Environmental Protection Agency, [Reporting requirements for oil discharges](https://www.epa.gov/oil-spills-prevention-and-preparedness-regulations/reporting-requirements-oil-discharges) | Confirmed that an oil discharge reaching U.S. waters and causing a film, sheen, discoloration, sludge, or emulsion is reportable to the National Response Center. |
| Motoring etiquette — safe speed, local/no-wake limits, and responsibility for wake near docks or moored boats | New York State Parks, Recreation and Historic Preservation, [Public Vessel Operator’s Guide](https://parks.ny.gov/sites/default/files/VesselOperatorsLicenseGuide2024.pdf), pp. 60–61 | Confirmed the general instruction to obey restrictions, reduce wake around marinas/docks/boats, and consider traffic and stopping distance. The lesson does not import New York’s specific numeric limits as universal rules. |

## Claims intentionally bounded

- Right-hand propeller means stern walk to port in reverse throughout this app,
  exactly matching eng-prop-walk-id, eng-prop-walk-use-docking, and
  custom-prop-walk.
- Pivot point is described only as commonly forward of amidships at low speed
  ahead. No fixed fraction or universal location is taught.
- Starting, shifting, trim, alarm, and cooling checks are generic operator
  awareness. The boat’s posted procedure and manufacturer manual control.
- Docking advice is a mental model, not a guaranteed maneuver. Wind, current,
  hull form, propeller, rudder, and available space determine the safe plan.
- Motoring etiquette does not create a marina-specific right-of-way rule.
